**Embedded Systems** is a system that combines computation with physical processes — where software and hardware interact with the physical world through sensors and actuators.

There is the concept of **Cyber-physical systems**, defined by Lee and Seshia as "an integration of computation with physical processes whose behavior is defined by both cyber and physical parts of the system.". This concept is a good way to understand the Embedded Systems.

## Primary textbook

<Card icon="open-book" title="Introduction to Embedded Systems — A Cyber-Physical Systems Approach">
  E. A. Lee and S. A. Seshia  
  *MIT Press, Second Edition, 2017*

  The course follows this book closely. Lee and Seshia frame embedded systems as **cyber-physical systems** — where computation is tightly coupled with physical processes, and where timing and concurrency are correctness criteria, not just performance concerns.
